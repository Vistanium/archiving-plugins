PASS when all of these hold:
1. It says the words were printed, and gives DRUM, April 1976, page 25, with a link, as the earliest printing found.
2. It credits them to Murtala Muhammed's speech at the OAU meeting on Angola.
3. It says the archive lacks the earlier issues (dailies or DRUM for January to March 1976), so this is the earliest printing in the archive, not necessarily the first anywhere.
FAIL if it says the words were never printed, or names a later page as the earliest.
